    A BETTER WAY TO IDENTIFY

    Don’t rely on one clue.

    Good identification comes from combining details: leaf or needle shape, how leaves attach to a twig, bark, cones or fruit, overall form, and where the tree is growing. A single feature can be misleading.

    • 01 Look at the whole tree before zooming in.
    • 02 Check several leaves, needles, or cones.
    • 03 Notice the habitat, elevation, and season.
